Terms of use

Please read these before using MedLink Uganda.

Not a substitute for medical care

MedLink provides information and connects you with health providers. The symptom checker and support chat give guidance only and do not diagnose. In an emergency call 999 or go to the nearest health facility.

Medicine information

Stock, price and verification results depend on data entered by facilities and registered batch records. A result of "not found" does not prove a medicine is fake. Ask a pharmacist or report it.

Your account

Keep your password private and give accurate information. Doctors, facilities and delivery companies are reviewed before approval and may be removed for misuse.

Payments

Wallet top-ups and payments are made through mobile money providers and are subject to their terms.
