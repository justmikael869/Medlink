Common questions

Do I need an account to use MedLink?

No. You can search for medicine, verify a batch, view doctors and take the anonymous mental health screening without signing in. An account is needed for bookings, your health wallet and records.

How does medicine verification work?

Enter the batch number on the pack. MedLink checks it against registered batches and tells you if it is verified, expired, recalled or not found, along with the manufacturer and expiry date where available.

Does the symptom checker replace a doctor?

No. It gives guidance on how urgently to seek care and suggests next steps. It does not diagnose. In an emergency, call 999 or go to the nearest facility.

Is the mental health screening really anonymous?

Yes. You can complete the PHQ-9 and GAD-7 and use the support chat without giving your name or logging in. If you choose to book a psychiatrist afterwards, you will need an account.

Can I use MedLink without internet?

Yes. Dial *280# on any phone to find medicine, book a doctor, check appointments and report shortages.

I run a pharmacy, clinic or delivery company. How do I join?

Choose your organisation type when you create an account. New facilities, doctors and delivery companies are reviewed before they go live.
