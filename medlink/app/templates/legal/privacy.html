Privacy

How MedLink Uganda handles your information.

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and phone number. If you use health features we also store what you choose to give us, such as appointments, prescriptions, your health record, pregnancy, vaccination or treatment schedules, and wallet transactions.

What we don't require

You can search for medicine, verify a medicine batch and take the mental health screening without an account. The screening and support chat do not ask for your name.

How we use it

We use your information to run the services you ask for: showing results, booking consultations, sending appointment and health reminders by SMS, and letting your doctor see the record you share with them. Facilities and district officers see aggregated stock and search information to plan supply.

Third-party services

Some features rely on outside providers, such as SMS and USSD delivery, video consultations, and AI-assisted symptom guidance and support chat. Only the information needed for that feature is sent to the provider.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account information. Contact the platform administrator using the details given to you when you registered.
